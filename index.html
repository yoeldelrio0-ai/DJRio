What goes into a Río set.

Layers 0/6
  1. Reads the room. He watches the floor and plays to the people on it, not to a script.
  2. Latin roots. Salsa, merengue, bachata and reggaeton from someone who grew up with them in San Juan.
  3. Custom edits and remixes. His own edits, so the songs you love land the way the room needs them.
  4. Seamless mixing. No dead air between tracks. The night keeps moving.
  5. Bilingual MC. Announcements and toasts in English and Spanish, so every guest feels included.
  6. Pro sound. Professional equipment, set up and tuned for your space.

From San Juan to Wilmington.

Yoel Del Rio was born in San Juan, Puerto Rico, and moved to North Carolina at 17. He earned a Business degree from UNCW and started out hosting Latin nights in Wilmington.

More than twenty years on, he plays weddings, corporate events, private parties and club nights, in English and Spanish. He also works with UNCW's Centro Hispano to promote Latin culture in the city.
